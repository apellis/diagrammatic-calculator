# Privacy policy

Signing in with Google gives this app your Google account ID, name and e-mail address. They are used only for your account and for sharing, and are not sold or disclosed except as hosting and e-mail delivery require.

They, your saved documents and the addresses you share with are stored on Cloudflare. A cookie keeps you signed in. Share notifications are sent through an e-mail provider.

Without signing in, documents stay in your browser.

An AI key you enter stays in your browser; AI requests go directly to the provider you chose.

This policy may change.
