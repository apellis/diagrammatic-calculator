# Privacy policy

Signing in is optional. Without it, nothing you enter is sent to us: your documents stay in your browser on your computer, which may delete them.

Signing in with Google is a convenience for keeping documents. It gives this app your Google account ID, name, and e-mail address. They are used only for your account and for sharing, and are not sold or disclosed except as hosting and e-mail delivery require.

These details, documents you save while signed in, and the addresses you share with are stored on Cloudflare. A cookie keeps you signed in. Share notifications are sent through an e-mail provider. You may request deletion of all your data at any time.

An optional AI API key you enter stays in your browser; AI requests go directly to the provider you chose.

This policy may change.
